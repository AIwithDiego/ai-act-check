# Pitch (60 to 90 seconds)

Every team shipping AI in Europe has the same question: is this allowed, is it high-risk, or is it fine? Today the answer costs a consultant and a week.

But the code is being written by agents now. So we let the agent ask, at the moment it matters, and pay for the answer.

[Demo: Claude Code, one sentence.] "We use an AI tool to shortlist CVs for our café chain." The agent pulls the free questionnaire, fills it in, calls the check, gets a 402 asking for fifty cents, pays over MPP, and gets the answer: high-risk, Annex III, employment, here's what you as the deployer have to do, and from when.

Two more: a website chatbot, that's a transparency case, you just tell people it's an AI. And software that reads your call-centre staff's emotions. That one's prohibited.

Three things we cared about:
- The agent never pays for a question we can't answer: bad input is rejected before the 402.
- The verdict is rule-based and cites the article, so what you pay for is checkable.
- We store nothing about your use case. The log has the tier and the receipt, that's it.

Not legal advice. A first pass that costs less than a coffee.
