AI Act Check

The live screen for the room at the MPP Hackathon Dublin. An agent pays 0.50 USD over Stripe MPP and the verdict lands here, next to the terminal, read from five to ten metres away. It extends the aiwithdiego.com site v5 system: same typeface, same colours, same marker. The site's night section becomes the stage. Every example on this page is drawn by public/tokens.css, the stylesheet the build links.

Design system v1, 9 Nov 2026. Source of the look: ~/Brand/website, app/(public)/site-v5.css.

00Principles

One answer per check

A keynote slide, not a dashboard. The tier is most of the screen; then what it means, why, and three things to do. The rest is a number.

One colour, one meaning

Hue belongs to the five tiers. Law, dates and roles are told apart by form (solid or dashed, filled or outlined), so a colour on screen always means a tier.

The marker is the arrival

A new verdict is revealed by the website's highlighter stroke at its own 34 ms a letter. Nothing else on the screen moves.

A slide, not a page

Type scales with --u from a 1920x1080 grid; the layout fills any viewport, 16:9 or not. Nothing scrolls.

03Colour-blind check

Simulated with Machado (2009) matrices. For protanopia and deuteranopia, prohibited, high-risk and minimal all drift towards ochre and minimal sits close to high-risk in hue. That is why no tier relies on hue: each has its own word, its own glyph (⊘ ▲ ⓘ ○ dashed ?) and a different lightness (hot 0.27, live 0.49, lilac 0.67, yellow 0.79 relative luminance). 29Accessibility

Contrast

Body ≥ 7:1 (text 15.77, text-2 10.79). Ink on every tier fill ≥ 5.94, all at large sizes. Rules are decorative; the incomplete outline is 6.52.

Not colour alone

Tier = word + glyph + lightness. Law = word + solid/dashed rule. Date = words + filled/outlined. Status = words + dot/ring.

Screen readers

One h1 (product), the answer is an h2, What you must do an h3. The screen is aria-live="polite": a new check reads as tier, sentence, why, duties, then the payment. Glyphs are aria-hidden.

Motion

The marker stroke is the only animation. prefers-reduced-motion removes it; the verdict appears whole. No flashing, no loops.

Forced colours

The marker becomes Highlight with HighlightText; glyphs, tick and dot become CanvasText.

Language and dates

lang="en". Dates as 2 Dec 2027, times 24 h Europe/Dublin. No interactive controls on the screen, so no focus order to manage; the focus ring (hot, 3px) exists for the plain page.
