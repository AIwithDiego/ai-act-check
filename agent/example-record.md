# AI Act check: AI shortlisting of CVs for café jobs

Date: 2026-11-10

Tier: High-risk

## Matched rules

- Employment and workers management (Art. 6(2), Annex III point 4)
- AI literacy (Art. 4)
- GDPR: lawful basis, transparency and security (GDPR Arts. 5, 6, 13, 14, 32)

## What you must do

### AI Act

- Train staff who run or use the AI, and keep a record. (Art. 4, applies from 2025-02-02)
- Use it according to the provider's instructions. (Art. 26(1), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Assign trained, competent people to oversee it. (Art. 26(2), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Make sure the input data you control is relevant to the purpose. (Art. 26(4), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Monitor it, and suspend it and tell the provider if it poses a risk. (Art. 26(5), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Keep the logs it generates for at least six months. (Art. 26(6), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Tell people when AI helps make decisions about them. (Art. 26(11), applies from 2027-12-02)
- Explain the decision to an affected person who asks. (Art. 86, applies from 2027-12-02)
- If you are a public body or provide a public service, do a fundamental rights impact assessment before first use. (Art. 27, applies from 2027-12-02)
- Before using it at work, inform workers' representatives and the affected workers. (Art. 26(7), applies from 2027-12-02)

### GDPR

- Pick and record a lawful basis for using the personal data. (GDPR Art. 6, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Tell people what data you use, why, for how long, and that AI is involved. (GDPR Arts. 13, 14, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Use only the personal data you need, and keep it no longer than needed. (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c), (e), applies from 2018-05-25)
- Let people access, correct and delete their data, and object. (GDPR Arts. 15-21, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Keep the personal data secure (access control, encryption, logging). (GDPR Art. 32, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Have a data processing agreement with every AI vendor that handles the personal data for you. (GDPR Art. 28(3), applies from 2018-05-25)
- If the data leaves the EEA (e.g. a US model API), use a transfer tool such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or SCCs. (GDPR Arts. 44-46, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Do a data protection impact assessment before you start, and review it when the system changes. (GDPR Art. 35, applies from 2018-05-25)
- If high risk remains after mitigation, consult the data protection authority before starting. (GDPR Art. 36, applies from 2018-05-25)
- Only make these decisions if needed for a contract, allowed by law, or with explicit consent. (GDPR Art. 22(2), applies from 2018-05-25)
- Let people get a human to review the decision, give their view and contest it. (GDPR Art. 22(3), applies from 2018-05-25)
- Explain the logic involved and the likely consequences to the people affected. (GDPR Arts. 13(2)(f), 15(1)(h), applies from 2018-05-25)
